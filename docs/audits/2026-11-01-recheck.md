## Independent uncertainty check

Method: invoke current `run_pipeline` with default 1,000-user seed-42 cohort and saved checkpoints, replacing `write_result` with a no-op. Capture the two per-user NDCG arrays from `mean_metric`; bootstrap their paired difference with 10,000 user resamples, RNG seed 721. Use one Torch/FAISS thread. These intervals condition on the existing selected models/cohort and do not account for test-set model selection or training variability.

Without safety, the rerun exactly reproduced the logged result. Absolute NDCG difference: **+0.0001753385**. Paired percentile-bootstrap 95% interval: **[-0.0011402728, +0.0014876899]**. Only **17/1,000** users have nonzero two-tower NDCG, versus **15/1,000** for popularity; 24 users have differing scores between systems. The interval includes zero and meaningful losses as well as gains. It does not establish a reliable positive effect.

With safety, the rerun also exactly reproduced the logged result. Absolute NDCG difference: **+0.0002389593**. Paired percentile-bootstrap 95% interval: **[-0.0010732096, +0.0015454518]**. Nonzero NDCG occurs for **18/1,000** two-tower users versus **15/1,000** popularity users; 24 have different scores. This interval also crosses zero, and the asymmetric filtering policy further limits interpretation.
